Programme Overview

About the Programme

The MBA program at CHRIST University is a rigorous, full-time two-year program structured across six trimesters, designed to develop future-ready business leaders with strong managerial, analytical, and leadership capabilities. The curriculum integrates foundational core courses with a wide range of specialization electives, enabling students to build both breadth and depth in management education.

The first two trimesters focus on strengthening students’ understanding of core management disciplines, including finance, marketing, human resources, operations, economics, analytics, and organizational behavior. In the subsequent trimesters, students can tailor their academic journey by choosing from six industry-relevant specializations — Finance, Marketing, Business Analytics, Human Resource, Lean Operations & Systems, and International Business — aligned with their career aspirations and professional interests.

The program emphasizes experiential and application-oriented learning through case-based pedagogy, simulations, gamification, live industry projects, field studies, and collaborative discussions. Students engage with real-world business challenges, enhancing their critical thinking, analytical reasoning, strategic decision-making, and problem-solving abilities. This immersive learning environment at the School of Business and Management (SBM) encourages active participation and industry relevance throughout the programme.

Beyond academic excellence, the program is designed to nurture socially responsible leaders with strong ethical values, self-awareness, and a global outlook. Students are exposed to international perspectives through global immersion opportunities and student exchange programs that broaden their understanding of contemporary business practices across cultures and markets.

SBM takes pride in its diverse and inclusive learning ecosystem, bringing together students from varied academic backgrounds, professional experiences, and cultural contexts. This diversity enriches peer learning and fosters collaborative growth. Supported by a strong network of industry leaders, corporate partners, and accomplished alums, students gain valuable exposure through corporate interface sessions, industry interactions, networking events, internships, and student-driven club activities, preparing them for successful managerial careers in a dynamic global business environment.

Message from the HOD

HOD BBA

At the School of Business and Management, our BBA H/HWR, BBA Decision Science, BBA Applied Finance with Fintech and Integrated MBA programmes are strategically designed to create future-ready professionals who can lead, innovate, and contribute meaningfully to society. With a strong emphasis on holistic development, the programmes go beyond classroom learning to nurture intellectual, emotional, social, and professional competencies among students. In alignment with the evolving global business landscape, we focus extensively on skill enhancement, critical thinking, analytical capabilities, and leadership development. Our curriculum and academic ecosystem are carefully structured to prepare students to navigate dynamic, uncertain, and technology-driven business environments with confidence and adaptability. Through experiential learning, industry engagement, live projects, and collaborative learning opportunities, students are encouraged to develop problem-solving abilities, strategic thinking, and decision-making skills essential for contemporary business leadership. Recognising the transformational role of Artificial Intelligence and emerging technologies, we integrate AI-enabled learning, data-driven decision making, and digital competencies into the student learning experience. At the same time, we strongly uphold ethics, integrity, moral values, and social responsibility as foundational pillars of management education. 

We believe that the leaders of tomorrow must combine technological competence with ethical sensitivity and human values. Entrepreneurship development is another strategic priority within our programmes. We actively cultivate an entrepreneurial mindset by encouraging innovation, creativity, opportunity recognition, and risk-taking abilities among students. Through incubation support, mentorship, industry interactions, business simulations, and entrepreneurial activities, students are empowered to become job creators, innovators, and socially responsible business leaders capable of contributing to economic and societal progress. Equal importance is given to curricular, co-curricular, and extra-curricular engagement to ensure well-rounded development. Leadership forums, management fests, clubs, social outreach initiatives, sports, cultural activities, community engagement, and professional development platforms provide students with opportunities to enhance teamwork, communication, leadership, and interpersonal skills. At SBM, we remain committed to delivering a transformative learning experience that develops competent professionals, visionary entrepreneurs, ethical leaders, and responsible citizens who can thrive and lead effectively in an increasingly complex and dynamic global environment.

Eligibility criteria
01
Candidates who have passed the Undergraduate degree examination with 50 % aggregate marks from any recognised University in India or abroad recognised by UGC / AIU are eligible to apply. Students writing their final degree examinations during March-June 2027 are also eligible to apply. The scores of the following tests (MAT / CAT / CMAT / XAT / ATMA / GMAT FOCUS / GRE) held in 2026 and 2027 will be accepted.
CAT Nov/Dec 2026 XAT 2027 GMAT 2026 / 2027 GRE 2026 / 2027 CMAT 2027 MAT Sep/Dec 2026/Feb 2027 ATMA 2027
02
To qualify for admission, candidates must have at least the minimum required score for their respective entrance exam. The minimum Score specified for MAT / CAT / CMAT / XAT / GMAT / GRE / ATMA is only eligibility criteria and the actual score thereof shall have no bearing in the selection process.
CAT — 60 Percentile XAT — 60 Percentile CMAT — 60 Percentile GRE — 295 CMAT — 60 Percentile MAT — 600 ATMA — 60 Percentile
03
Candidates applying for the Dual Degree MBA (CU) + MBA, THWS Germany must submit work-related documents — Joining Letter or Experience Letter with latest Salary Slip. Full-time employment and family business are both considered. Minimum one year of work experience is mandatory.

THWS Eligibility
    Students should have a minimum score of 2.5 GPA (German grade conversion) in their undergraduate program.
    1-year professional work experience is mandatory to apply to this program
    Students should clear German language A1 before the completion of the MBA program
04
For MBA (CU) + MBA (WMU, USA): Students must also obtain a minimum composite score of 450 in GMAT or 295 in GRE before students formally apply for WMU admission which will be during September 2027. (Mandatory).
