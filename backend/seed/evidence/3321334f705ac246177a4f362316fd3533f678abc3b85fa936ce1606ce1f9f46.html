Bachelors' Programmes

In our undergraduate (UG) programmes, IIT Bombay has been at the forefront of implementing cutting-edge teaching methodologies and courses. We've recently updated our curriculum to align with 21st-century student needs. Key changes include:

  • Introduction of hands-on courses like maker space
  • Early integration of data science and AI courses
  • Increased flexibility in course selection, both within and outside of departments
  • More non-technical courses to foster well-rounded graduates
  • Students can also opt for minor programmes (refer to the UG rule book).

Dual Degree

The Dual Degree program (DD) is a 5-year program where, upon successful completion of the necessary academic requirements, a student is awarded two Degrees. B.Tech. Degree in Electrical Engineering and M.Tech. Degree with Specialization in (e.g. Communication and Signal Processing).

Admission to IIT Bombay's 4- and 5-year undergraduate programmes is through the exams listed below. For more information on eligibility criteria and other details, please visit the provided links below.

  • Joint Entrance Examination-Advanced (JEE-Advanced), Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSSA)
  • Undergraduate Common Entrance Exam for Design (UCEED)
  • Indian National Mathematical Olympiad (INMO). IIT Bombay admits a few students who qualify Indian National Mathematical Olympiad (INMO). 

Interested students are encouraged to visit the webpages of their chosen departments to explore the scope of study, available courses, faculty profiles, research activities, and other relevant information.
