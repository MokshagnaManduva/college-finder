Symbiosis International (Deemed University) is a multi-disciplinary university offering its students and faculty a vibrant learning ecosystem designed around its multi-cultural and innovative ethos.

Symbiosis was established in 1971 by Prof. Dr. S.B. Mujumdar, which was a ‘home away from home’ for International students. The Institution is based on the principles of Vedic thought of "World is one family".

Research Centres

The University has strong research credentials which is reflected through the number of its Ph.D. students, Publications, Patents and R&D Projects granted by Government and Non-government organizations. With well-equipped laboratories and research centers in fields like applied artificial intelligence, medical imaging, behavioral science, stem cell research, urban studies, climate change and sustainability, waste management, and nanotechnology, the University fosters a vibrant research culture.
