Gateway to Discover, Connect & Learn

The BITS Pilani library is housed in a state-of-the-art building, covering about 65000 sq.ft area and is located close to all academic blocks of the Institute. The library indeed is an architectural marvel with an attractive palatial interiors high ceiling adequate natural lighting and ventilation with several series of courtyards, with each used for different activities. From the Desk of Director

"The only thing that you absolutely have to know, is the location of the library" - Albert Einstein People come to the library for reading, study, research, brainstorming, discussion, innovation, reference and sometimes socializing in virtual and/or real form. Library is the knowledge ocean in the form of printed materials, sound and video recordings, maps, photographs, computer software, online databases, internet of things and other media. I am sure that every visitor will have a great experience of pilgrimage while visiting this library. Enjoy in the midst of the rich resources as well as collaborative spaces and touch the sky of the knowledge on the ground here! Prof. Sudhirkumar Barai Director, BITS Pilani, Pilani Campus

From the Desk of Vice-Chancellor

BITS Pilani's Library, a beacon in the rich mosaic of our academic environment, softly narrates stories of enlightenment and sparks a flame of wonder. Echoing Ralph Waldo Emerson's words, "The library is not just metaphorically but actually the essence of a university." Within its sacred walls, academic finds its expression, research soars, and intellects feast on the rich lore of myriad narratives. It's a place where history sheds light on today, and tomorrow's tales are told one leaf at a time. With its continuously growing digital domain and meticulously assembled collections, our library surpasses the physical confines, evolving into an infinite repository open to everyone. As we step into this new scholastic year, let's pledge to value this repository of riches nature a spirit of exploration, and preserve the library as the pulsating core of our scholarly pursuits. In unity, we shall explore its chapters, discover its truths, and inscribe our own saga in the enduring pursuit of wisdom. Happy Learning. Prof. V. Ramgopal Rao Group Vice-Chancellor, BITS Pilani
